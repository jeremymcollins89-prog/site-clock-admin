Catalog

Recurring charges you can drop into a quote or invoice's line items with one click, instead of retyping them.

Quotes

Quotes are estimates for work that hasn't been booked yet, emailed to the customer automatically as soon as you save one. Once a customer accepts, convert the quote into a scheduled job and/or an invoice -- no need to retype anything.

Invoices

Invoices are created as drafts, then emailed to the customer once you click Send. The email includes a Pay now button for online card/bank payments, which marks the invoice paid automatically. Use Mark Paid yourself only for payments that came in another way (cash, check, a card run elsewhere).

Transactions

Every paid invoice -- in-person/phone payments you recorded yourself, and online payments collected automatically through Stripe.

Expenses

Materials, insurance, rent, or any other business cost -- logged here so Net Profit on the Reports tab reflects what you actually spent, not just labor.

Reports

Labor hours, invoiced totals, paid totals, and profit for a time range.

Get paid online

Connect your own Stripe account so customer payments on your invoices go straight to your bank account, not ours. Stripe's own processing fees apply (2.9%+30¢ card / 0.8% bank transfer), plus a 0.5% platform fee per payment.

Connected Payments go directly to your Stripe account.
Finish connecting in the browser tab that just opened, then come back and reopen Settings to see it marked as connected.

Payroll email

Where "Submit Hours for Payroll" sends timesheet emails from your employees. Required before employees can submit hours.

Long shift alert

Get a notification if an employee is still clocked in past this many hours -- handy for catching a forgotten clock-out.

Pay schedule

How often your pay periods repeat. This controls what "Submit Hours for Payroll" bundles together.

Pick the first day of any one pay period you already know (e.g. the start of last pay period). Every period is calculated from this date.

Change admin email

This is the email you sign into this app with. Changing it requires your current password.

Business timezone

Where this business is located. Used to send automated emails (like invoice reminders) at a sensible local hour instead of a fixed one.

Shop location

This is the location employees auto clock-in at when they arrive (and auto clock-out from when they leave). Tip: right-click your shop's spot in Google Maps, click the coordinates that pop up to copy them, then paste the first number below as Latitude and the second as Longitude.

Employees won't get auto clocked in on arrival before this time, even if they're at the shop. Leave at 12:00 AM for no restriction.

Employees still clocked in "In Town" get auto clocked out at this time if they've left the shop. Doesn't apply to "Traveling" shifts.
